Regulation M-C

The current regulation is called Regulation M-C for Pokemon Champions. This season added a new effect called Terrains and added new items and Mega evolutions.

Team Archetypes

  • Balance
  • Hyper Offense
  • Weather
  • Hard Trick Room
  • Stall

These are some of the popular team types that you may see in the tournament results of officially held competitions.

Quick Explanations

Balance has an even amount of defensive and offensive Pokemon combined with 1-2 support Pokemon that provide recovery, speed control, or redirection. Hyper Offense is fully focused around fast damage dealers. Tailwind, Choice Scarf and Pokemon that pressure big damage immediately. Weather teams focus around one of four weather types; Rain, Snow, Sandstorm, or Sun. You stack Pokemon on that team that benefit from the damage boost, activating abilities, and other Pokemon that gain advantage from being in the weather. Hard Trick Room teams build around slow and strong Pokemon. On turn 1 they set up trick room to allow the sweepers to take control of the game. Stall teams are defensive walls. They focus on inflicting poison or other means of damage over time while pivoting around recovery, protecting, and making the game go as long as possible to rack up the damage and knockouts.

Top Teams

At the 2027 Baltimore Pokemon Regional Championship, a player by the name of Joseph Ugarte won the VGC finals with a hyper offense team. He used two of the strongest mega evolutions in Salamence and Tyranitar. He used pokemon with naturally high speed stats, choice scarf, and a Pokemon that doubles it's speed in the sandstorm weather. This Hyper Offensive team used its offensive pressure to take the meta apart and claim victory at Baltimore Regionals.
