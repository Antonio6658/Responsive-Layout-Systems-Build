Resource Guide

Nintendo Switch 2 / Mobile

Pokemon VGC is played on a platform called Pokemon Champions! This can be downloaded and played for free on the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, or any modern mobile device!

Important disclaimer

In officially held tournaments; Regional, International, and Worlds, you cannot use a mobile device. Champions must be played on a Nintendo product!
